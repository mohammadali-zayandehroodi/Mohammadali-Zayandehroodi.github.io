About

I am Mohammadali Zayandehroodi, a transportation engineering researcher working on traffic safety, machine learning, and transportation systems modeling.

I received my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology in 2025. My doctoral research focused on ranking and selecting safety countermeasures at highway–rail grade crossings.

My research experience includes visiting positions at ETH Zurich and Penn State University. At ETH Zurich, I worked on generating synthetic rear-end crash scenarios.

Research interests

  • Transportation safety and highway–rail grade crossings
  • Machine learning and uncertainty modeling
  • Traffic signal control and optimization
  • Transportation systems modeling and simulation
  • Large language models for transportation

Teaching

Teaching Traffic Engineering at Shahid Bahonar University of Kerman since October 2025, as listed in my CV.
