About my research

I received my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology in 2025. My doctoral research focused on ranking and selecting safety countermeasures at highway–rail grade crossings.

My research experience includes visiting positions at ETH Zurich and Penn State University. At ETH Zurich, I worked on generating synthetic rear-end crash scenarios.
